MTCP

The Military Training and Cooperation Program (MTCP) is a Department of National Defence program that supports international defence relationships through education, training, capacity building, and military assistance.

The program provides opportunities for member nations to participate in professional development courses and capacity building activities in Canada and abroad.

MTCP Objectives

  • Strengthening bilateral and multilateral cooperation with Canada’s partners
  • Expanding and reinforcing Canada's defence relationships
  • Promoting democratic principals internationally including rule of law, protection of human rights and respect for diversity and gender
  • Influencing areas of strategic interest to Canada
  • Building partner capacity at the defence institution and security force levels
  • Delivering enhanced military assistance during crisises when directed by Cabinet and/or the Prime Minister

What we do

The Military Training and Cooperation Program (MTCP) works with trusted partners to assist building and development of professional, capable, and interperable armed forces in its member nations.

MTCP provides:

  • Defence education and training
  • Capacity building support
  • Military assistance including equipment donations
This work helps strengthen skills, leadership and instituional capabilities of partner nations. These nations are better prepared to prevent crises, respond effectively to challenges and contribute to regional and global stability.

Program Adminstration

MTCP is managed by two distinct directorates under the Directorate General - Cooperation Assistance and Training: the Directorate of Military Training and Cooperation (DMTC) and the Directorate of Military Assistance and Cooperation
